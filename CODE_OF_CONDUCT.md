# Code of Conduct

LocalDrop follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
v2.1. In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good intent, and keep
feedback technical and actionable.

- **Our pledge:** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
  background or identity.
- **Our standards:** empathetic language; constructive criticism; grace
  toward mistakes; focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- **Unacceptable:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, unwelcome
  attention, or publishing others' private information.
- **Enforcement:** report issues privately to the maintainer (see git log for
  contact). Violations may lead to warnings, temporary bans, or permanent
  removal from project spaces.

If a discussion gets heated, pause, restate the technical question, and
continue tomorrow. Shipping good software is the goal; kindness is the method.
